Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is buccal fat reduction and who is an ideal candidate?

Buccal fat reduction is a precise surgical procedure that removes excess fat pads from the cheeks. It is ideal for individuals who feel they have a rounder or "chubby" face that does not change despite weight loss and who desire a more sculpted look.

2. How long does recovery take after cheek fat removal surgery?

Most patients experience initial swelling and bruising that subsides within 1 to 2 weeks. You can typically return to light daily activities and work within a few days, following post-op care guidance from your plastic surgeon.

3. Will facial contour enhancement leave visible scars?

No. For buccal fat removal and many standard facial plastic procedures, incisions are carefully placed inside the mouth or hidden within natural facial creases, ensuring no visible external scarring.

4. Are the results of a defined cheekbones procedure permanent?

Yes. Once the buccal fat pads are removed or facial contours are surgically refined, those fat cells do not grow back, providing long-lasting, beautifully defined facial contours.

5. How do I know if I am choosing the right plastic surgeon?

Look for a board-certified plastic surgeon with specialized experience in facial aesthetics, a proven portfolio of successful patient outcomes, and a consultation process that prioritizes your safety, comfort, and custom goals.
